Contributions created with the help of generative AI models are heavily discouraged. Workshop.codes is a platform that fosters and encourages creators. AI models are build on the wonderful, silly, open, and hard work of all artists alike, with the goal of profiting of and pushing out those same artists. These models can not in good conscience be considered helpful or part of the greater good, as their supposed help is a doubled edged sword. Workshop.codes wishes to nourish creation, not replace it. If you do use generative models in your flow we ask you to reconsider.
